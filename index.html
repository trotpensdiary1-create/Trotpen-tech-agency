Questions, answered

Can't find your question? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start from ₦80,000. The final price depends on pages, features, content and complexity. Message us your requirements for a clear quote.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly content and feedback come in. We agree a timeline before we start.

Do you build websites for small businesses?

Yes. Small and medium-sized local businesses are our main focus, starting in Abuja.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website is built mobile-first and tested on smartphones, tablets and desktops.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. We add WhatsApp buttons with a pre-filled message so customers reach you in one tap.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We modernise the design, improve the structure and make it work better on mobile.

Can you help me put my website online?

Yes. Deployment is included in every package, and we can help you set up hosting and a domain.

Do I need to provide my own domain?

No. You can bring your own, or we can help you choose and set one up.
