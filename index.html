Questions, Answered

How much does a website cost?

Packages start from ₦80,000. The final price depends on the number of pages, features, content and complexity. Message us with your requirements for a clear quote.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly content and feedback come in. A simple website is usually faster than a larger one with more features. We'll agree a timeline before we start.

Do you build websites for small businesses?

Yes. Small and medium-sized local businesses are our main focus, starting in Abuja.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website is built mobile-first and tested to work on smartphones, tablets and desktops.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. We add WhatsApp buttons with a pre-filled message so customers can reach you in one tap.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can modernise the design, improve the structure and make it work better on mobile.

Can you help me put my website online?

Yes. Deployment is included in every package, and we can help you set up hosting and a domain.

Do I need to provide my own domain?

No. You can bring your own domain, or we can help you choose and set one up.
